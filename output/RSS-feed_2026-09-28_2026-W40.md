> arXiv:2609.23185v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Lossy compression of scientific simulation data increasingly relies on learned, latent-space architectures such as Residual Vector Quantization (RVQ), which iteratively quantize a base representation and its residuals to progressively reduce reconstruction error. While effective, RVQ performs this residual modeling entirely in latent space, leaving the pixel-space error structure of the reconstruction largely unaddressed. In this work, we propose a post-processing pipeline that augments an RVQ-based compressor with a U-Net trained to predict and correct pixel-space residuals between the original volume and its RVQ reconstruction. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first pipeline to combine latent-space RVQ, explicit pixel-space residual correction via a deep spatial post-processing network, and GAE-based error-bound guarantees within a single framework for scientific data compression.
